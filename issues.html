Mermazine: For the merfolk of East Anglia!

It is here! A folklore magazine - which is more like a book - which celebrates the Mermaids of East Anglia, whether found in folklore, church, art or story. This is our first issue of many, for there are many mermaids to discover!

"The Material we place before you in this little magazine all flows from the wellspring of inspiration offered by the many mermaids inhabiting churches, village signs, murals and stories throughout Norfolk, Suffolk and Cambridgeshire. From coastal characters of wave and salt, and the Fen wind to the devious Mbuq and farr, each individual has their own song. Their own message, their own contributions to the magic of the landscape, whether or not the humans that are there actively notice their presence."

Created by Johnny Penn, Val Thomas, Chris Wood and Alice Kerridge-Crick
(The Mermaids of Fensalir Cunning)

Each issue will be a community endeavour with this issue having contributions by:

Imogen Ashwin - Magical practitioner and weaver of stories who you can find via her blog 'Bracken & Wrack' on Substack.

Matt Willis - Illustrator and co-founder of Shuck Zine and the owner of "The Folklore Box" which you can find here! www.thefolklorebox.com and via Instagram.

This magazine has been designed to be accessible to all with care taken to choose a clear font and good size print for ease of reading.

The magazine is A5 size and has a page count of 118 pages (so it is a book!)

Though Mermazine is about the Merfolk of East Anglia it is written for all to enjoy, from all over the world, especially if you love folklore, magic, history, the environment and of course Mermaids. It is our wish that this will encourage the reader to explore their own surroundings and the magic held within it.
